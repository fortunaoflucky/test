📋 Game Guide:

Controls:

Desktop: Arrows/WASD to move, Space to shoot

Mobile: Tilt device to move, Tap to shoot

Block Types:

🔵 Water: No passage, bullets pass through

🟢 Bush: Hide tanks, bullets pass through

🟤 Brick: 3 hits to destroy

Tank Levels:

Tank shape changes with level (more angles)

Higher level = more health & faster fire

Kills increase level, damage decreases it

🎯 Objective: Work with your team to destroy enemy tanks. Maps change every 30 seconds. Tank respawns in 5 seconds after death.
